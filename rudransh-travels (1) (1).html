Common Questions

Travel FAQ

What is the best time for Adi Kailash Yatra? ↗
Late May to June (snow & flora) or September to October (crystal-clear views). We avoid July–August due to monsoon road hazards in Kumaon.
How are Inner Line Permits arranged? ↗
We handle the complete ILP process through Dharchula SDM. You need Aadhar Card, 4 passport photos, and a Police Verification Certificate or valid Passport.
Is the journey physically demanding? ↗
4×4 vehicles now reach Jolingkong, but 14,000ft+ altitude requires stamina. We recommend 2 weeks of light cardio prep before the trip.
What vehicles are used for the expedition? ↗
High-clearance 4×4 Boleros and Force Travelers maintained for Himalayan terrain, each equipped with professional oxygen cylinders and first-aid kits.
What adventure sports are best for first-timers? ↗
Tandem paragliding, Grade 2-3 river rafting on the Saryu, and guided Khaliya Top trek are perfect for adventure beginners visiting Pithoragarh.
What is the cancellation/refund policy? ↗
Weather or government order cancellations receive full credit for the next batch. For mid-trip partial cancellations, we provide transparent pro-rata refunds.
