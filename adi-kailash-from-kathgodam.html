Kathgodam Package — Train-Friendly 6-Day Yatra

The Kathgodam package is our most popular choice for pilgrims travelling from Delhi, UP, and North India by train. Kathgodam (NKE) is the railhead for Kumaon — we pick you up directly at the station and handle everything from there.

Over 6 days and 5 nights, you complete the full pilgrimage: Pithoragarh base, Vyas Valley, Adi Kailash darshan at Jyolingkong (4,570m), Om Parvat darshan at Nabidhang (4,246m), Pithoragarh local exploration, and a scenic return via Jageshwar Dham, Chitai Golu Devta, and Kainchi Dham.

Prefer a different starting point? Check our budget-friendly Pithoragarh Package (3D · ₹18,000) if you are already in Uttarakhand, or our all-inclusive Delhi Door-to-Door Package (6D · ₹35,000). For comprehensive route maps and holy significance, explore our Master Adi Kailash & Om Parvat Guide.

🚂 Pick-up Point: Kathgodam Railway Station (NKE) — nearest major rail terminus to Kumaon. Overnight trains from Delhi reach Kathgodam in 6–8 hours.

6-Day Itinerary — Kathgodam Route

Day 1: Kathgodam → Pithoragarh (Transit)

Day 1

Our driver picks you up at Kathgodam Railway Station (NKE). Drive through the scenic Kumaon foothills — passing Bhimtal, Almora, and the mountain roads — to arrive at Pithoragarh by afternoon/evening (approx. 260 km, 6 hrs). Check in to your hotel in Pithoragarh. Briefing by our team on the yatra plan, ILP, and what to expect at altitude. Overnight stay in Pithoragarh.

Distance: ~260 km | Drive: ~6 hrs | Night halt: Pithoragarh hotel

Day 2: Pithoragarh → Nabi / Gunji via Dharchula

Day 2

Depart early morning from Pithoragarh. Drive along the Kali River through Dharchula (915m), clearing ITBP checkpoints with your Inner Line Permit. Ascend through the breathtaking Chialekh Pass into the pristine Vyas Valley. Arrive at the high-altitude village of Nabi or Gunji (3,200m) by afternoon. Check in to an authentic Bhotiya tribal homestay. Rest and acclimatize. Overnight in Nabi/Gunji.

Distance: ~180 km | Drive: ~8 hrs | Night halt: Nabi/Gunji homestay (3,200m)

Day 3: Jyolingkong — Adi Kailash Darshan (4,570m)

Day 3

Drive from Nabi/Gunji to Jyolingkong (4,570m / 15,000 ft). Witness the majestic Adi Kailash (Chhota Kailash) peak in all its glory. Visit the sacred Parvati Sarovar lake, Gauri Kund, and the ancient Parvati temple. Perform puja rituals with our local pandit. Absorb the divine stillness of this sacred valley. Return to Nabi/Gunji homestay for dinner and overnight rest.

Max altitude: 4,570m | Night halt: Nabi/Gunji

Day 4: Om Parvat Darshan (4,246m) → Return to Pithoragarh

Day 4

Early morning drive to Nabidhang (4,246m). Witness the miraculous naturally formed "ॐ" snow symbol on Om Parvat's face — one of the most spiritually charged sights in the Himalayas. After this sacred darshan, begin the long descent drive back through Chialekh Pass and Dharchula, arriving at Pithoragarh by evening. Overnight at hotel in Pithoragarh.

Max altitude: 4,246m | Night halt: Pithoragarh hotel

Day 5: Pithoragarh Local Exploration (Leisure Day)

Day 5

A well-deserved leisure day to explore Pithoragarh — Uttarakhand's "Mini Kashmir." Visit Pithoragarh Fort (British-era), Mostamanu Temple (local deity), Chandak Hills viewpoint, and the colourful Simalgair Bazaar. Shop for local Himalayan handicrafts, woollens, and spices. Optional: visit Dhwaj Temple or Kapileshwar Mahadev. Overnight in Pithoragarh.

Night halt: Pithoragarh hotel

Day 6: Return to Kathgodam via Jageshwar, Chitai Golu & Kainchi Dham

Day 6

Depart Pithoragarh after breakfast. Drive via Almora to the ancient cluster of 124 stone Shiva temples at Jageshwar Dham. Seek blessings at the sacred bell-laden Chitai Golu Devta temple. Visit Kainchi Dham — the famous ashram of Neem Karoli Baba (Maharaj-ji), revered worldwide. Continue to Kathgodam station. Yatra ends at Kathgodam — board your train back home carrying the spiritual blessings of the sacred peaks.

Distance: ~260 km | Drive: ~7 hrs | Yatra ends at Kathgodam station

Inner Line Permit — We Handle Everything

An Inner Line Permit (ILP) is mandatory for the Adi Kailash route. We manage the complete application through the Dharchula SDM office on your behalf.

Documents needed (submit 15 days in advance):

  • Aadhaar Card — colour copy (both sides)
  • Police Verification Certificate (PCC)
  • 4 recent passport-size photographs (physical)
  • Medical fitness certificate from registered MBBS doctor

We submit, track, and carry your permit for all ITBP checkpoints. Full guide: ILP for Adi Kailash 2026.
