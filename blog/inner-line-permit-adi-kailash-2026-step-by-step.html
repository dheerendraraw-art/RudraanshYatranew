Inner Line Permit for Adi Kailash — Step-by-Step 2026

Documents • Application Process • Timeline • Fees • Common Mistakes to Avoid

By Rudraansh Yatra Team • August 2026 • 8 min read

The Adi Kailash Yatra route passes through the Vyas Valley — a restricted border area in Pithoragarh district that runs along the India-China and India-Nepal borders. All Indian pilgrims are required to obtain an Inner Line Permit (ILP) before entering this zone.

Getting the ILP right is critical — a missing document, incorrect photograph format, or late submission can result in your permit being rejected and your yatra cancelled at the ITBP checkpoint in Dharchula. This guide covers the complete process from documents to collection.

What is an Inner Line Permit?

An Inner Line Permit (ILP) is an official travel document issued by the Government of India that allows Indian citizens to visit certain restricted border areas. For the Adi Kailash Yatra, the ILP is required because the route passes through Vyas Valley, which is designated as a restricted area under the Foreigners (Protected Areas) Order, 1958.

The permit is issued by the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) office in Dharchula, Pithoragarh. ITBP (Indo-Tibetan Border Police) checkpoints verify the permits at multiple points along the route — at Dharchula, and in Vyas Valley.

Documents Required for Adi Kailash ILP

Aadhaar CardColour photocopy — front and back on a single A4 sheet. Must be legible.
Police Verification CertificateIssued by your local police station. Shows you are a law-abiding citizen. Takes 7-10 days to obtain.
4 Passport-Size PhotographsRecent (within 6 months), white background, physical prints (not printouts). Size: 3.5cm x 4.5cm.
Medical Fitness CertificateSigned by an MBBS doctor. Must state you are fit for high-altitude travel above 4,000m. Includes blood pressure and heart health.
⚠️ Important: PCC Takes 7-10 Working DaysThe Police Verification Certificate is the document that takes the longest to obtain. Contact your nearest police station immediately after booking and request a PCC well in advance. Total ILP processing time: 5-7 days after all documents received.

Step-by-Step ILP Application Process (When Booked with Rudraansh Yatra)

1

Book Your Package and Confirm Dates

Choose your Adi Kailash package and confirm travel dates with us. You need a confirmed booking date to process the ILP — permits are date-specific.

2

Apply for Police Verification Certificate (PCC)

Visit your nearest police station or the police sub-division office and apply for a PCC. Carry your Aadhaar and 2 photographs. PCC takes 7-10 working days. Start this immediately after booking.

3

Get Medical Fitness Certificate

Visit an MBBS doctor (government or private hospital). Inform them you need a certificate for high-altitude travel (4,000m+). The certificate should mention your BP readings, basic fitness, and that you are medically cleared for altitude.

Common ILP Rejection Reasons — Avoid These Mistakes

  • Photographs not on white background — even a cream or off-white background causes rejection
  • PCC not from correct jurisdiction — must be from your permanent address police station, not current city
  • Medical certificate without MBBS stamp — only MBBS-registered doctors' certificates accepted
  • Aadhaar photocopy not legible — ensure both sides are clear, not overexposed or blurry
  • Submitting documents less than 7 days before departure — insufficient time for processing

Can Foreigners Do Adi Kailash Yatra?

As of 2026, the Vyas Valley route is restricted to Indian nationals only. Foreign nationals (including NRIs with foreign passports) cannot obtain an ILP for this route due to its proximity to the India-China border. NRIs with valid Indian passports or OCI cards should check current regulations with us before booking.
