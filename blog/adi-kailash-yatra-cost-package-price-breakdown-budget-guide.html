### Item-by-Item Expense Breakdown (If Planning Independently)

If you are curious about where the money goes, here is the realistic cost of each component:

#### 1. 4x4 Mahindra Bolero Transport (The Major Cost)

**Cost:** ₹6,500 – ₹8,500 per day for vehicle hire beyond Dharchula.

**Why it matters:** The route from Dharchula to Gunji, Jyolingkong, and Nabidhang has river crossings and unpaved mountain climbs. Only commercial 4x4 Boleros driven by native Pithoragarh mountain drivers are permitted by district transport authorities.

#### 2. High-Altitude Homestays in Gunji / Nabi

**Cost:** ₹1,500 – ₹2,500 per person per night (including heavy warm bedding, solar power, and home-cooked vegetarian meals).

**Experience:** Traditional wooden Rung Bhotiya homestays provide warm hospitality, local herbal teas (Mandua, Jambu soup), and insulated sleeping areas vital for surviving near-freezing night temperatures.

#### 3. Inner Line Permit (ILP) & Processing Fees

**Cost:** Approx. ₹500 – ₹1,200 (including administrative fee, notary, police verification stamp, and SDM documentation charges).

*Note: When booking with Rudraansh Yatra, all permit fees and direct processing with the Dharchula SDM office are 100% included in the package price.*

#### 4. Medical Fitness & Emergency Oxygen Setup

**Cost:** ₹300 – ₹500 for registered doctor's fitness certificate; ₹1,500 – ₹2,500 for portable emergency oxygen canister rentals.

### Tips to Save on Your 2026 Yatra

1. **Travel in a Group of 4 to 6:** 4x4 Bolero costs are optimized when 4 to 6 people share the vehicle, bringing down per-person costs significantly.

2. **Book 30–60 Days Early:** Homestays in Gunji and Nabi have limited capacity (only ~200 beds per day across the valley). Early booking locks in standard rates without peak-season surge pricing.

3. **Start from Pithoragarh or Kathgodam:** If you can take an overnight train from Delhi to Kathgodam yourself, you save ₹5,000+ per person compared to booking an expensive private taxi from Delhi.
