Adi Kailash Yatra Complete Guide 2026

Route • Cost • Inner Line Permit • Itinerary • Best Season • Packing List

By Rudraansh Yatra Team • August 2026 • 12 min read

The Adi Kailash Yatra — also called Chhota Kailash Yatra — is one of the most spiritually powerful pilgrimages in the Indian Himalayas. Every year, thousands of devotees make their way to the remote Vyas Valley in Pithoragarh, Uttarakhand to receive darshan of Adi Kailash (4,570m) and the miraculous Om Parvat (4,246m).

What is Adi Kailash?

Adi Kailash (literally "first Kailash") is a sacred Himalayan peak in the Vyas Valley of Pithoragarh district, revered as the earthly replica of Mt. Kailash in Tibet — the abode of Lord Shiva. The sacred lake at its base, Parvati Sarovar (Gauri Kund), mirrors the holy Mansarovar.

The pilgrimage also includes darshan of Om Parvat, where the divine "OM" symbol appears naturally formed in snow on the mountain face — considered one of the most miraculous sights in the Himalayas. For pilgrims who cannot undertake the arduous Mt. Kailash Yatra in Tibet, Adi Kailash offers an equivalent spiritual experience entirely within India.

Inner Line Permit (ILP) — Complete Process

The Adi Kailash route passes through the restricted border area near the India-China border. All pilgrims require an Inner Line Permit (ILP) from the SDM office in Dharchula.

Documents Required for ILP

  • Aadhaar Card — colour scan, both sides
  • Police Verification Certificate (PCC) — from your local police station
  • 4 Passport-Size Photographs — recent, physical copies
  • MBBS Medical Fitness Certificate — certifying fitness for high altitude

Day-by-Day Itinerary (6-Day Full Package)

  1. Day 1: Arrive Pithoragarh (or depart Delhi/Kathgodam overnight). ILP documents verified. Hotel check-in.
  2. Day 2: Drive Pithoragarh to Nabi/Gunji via Dharchula and Chialekh Pass (3,200m). ITBP checkpoint. Bhotiya homestay. Acclimatize.
  3. Day 3: Drive to Jyolingkong (4,570m). Adi Kailash (Chhota Kailash) darshan. Visit Parvati Sarovar, Gauri Kund, temple puja. Return to Nabi/Gunji.
  4. Day 4: Drive to Nabidhang (4,246m). Om Parvat darshan — witness the sacred OM in snow. Return to Pithoragarh.
  5. Day 5: Rest day in Pithoragarh. Visit Pithoragarh Fort, Mostamanu Temple, Chandak Hills, Simalgair Bazaar.
  6. Day 6: Return journey via Jageshwar Dham, Chitai Golu Devta, and Kainchi Dham en route to Kathgodam or Delhi.

Altitude and Health Guidelines

Maximum altitude on this yatra is 4,570m at Jyolingkong. The entire yatra is drive-based — no trekking required. But altitude can affect unprepared pilgrims:

  • Acclimatize: Spend one night in Nabi/Gunji (3,200m) before ascending to 4,570m
  • Hydrate: Drink 3-4 litres of water daily at altitude
  • No alcohol: Worsens altitude sickness significantly
  • AMS signs: Headache, nausea, dizziness — descend immediately
  • Oxygen cylinders: Carried per vehicle by Rudraansh Yatra for all groups
  • Diamox: Consult your doctor about acetazolamide prophylaxis before departure

Packing List for Adi Kailash Yatra

  • Warm thermal inner layers
  • Fleece jacket + down jacket (mandatory above 3,000m)
  • Waterproof outer shell / rain jacket
  • Trekking shoes with ankle support
  • Warm gloves, woollen cap, sun hat
  • UV-protection sunglasses
  • Personal medications + basic first-aid
  • ORS rehydration sachets
  • Power bank (limited charging in Vyas Valley)
  • Cash in small denominations (no ATMs in valley)
  • Government photo ID (Aadhaar / Passport)

Is Adi Kailash Yatra Suitable for Senior Citizens?

Yes — since the entire yatra is drive-based with no trekking, it is suitable for seniors in reasonably good health.
