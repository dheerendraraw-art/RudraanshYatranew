Transparent Pricing

Tour Inclusions & Ethics

Every yatra and trek details what is covered. No hidden surcharges, pure local hospitality.

What You Get

Fully Managed Local Logistics

Because we operate directly from Pithoragarh, our packages are all-inclusive of standard ground logistics, leaving you free to focus on your adventure and spiritual connection.

Premium Local Homestays

Cozy, clean local homestays in border villages (like Nabi and Gunji on the Adi Kailash Yatra route, or Tidang in Darma Valley) and high-quality double-layered alpine tents. We prioritize clean beds and local community hospitality.

Organic Local Meals

Nutritious, freshly cooked organic local Kumaoni and Indian vegetarian meals (dal, rice, local greens, rotis). In high altitudes, we ensure simple, easily digestible, healthy food.

Certified Guides & Staff

Native mountain guides certified in first aid, alongside experienced cooks, porters, and horse-handlers who understand high-altitude safety and border paths.

Inner Line Permits

All Inner Line Permits, border administrative clearances from the SDM office, forest department entry tickets, and environmental fees are handled entirely by us.

Rugged 4x4 Transportation

Reliable high-ground-clearance 4x4 vehicles (like Mahindra Boleros) starting right from Pithoragarh to handle the rough mountain roads of the borderlands safely.

Medical & Oxygen Safety

Every high-altitude expedition carries standard medical oxygen cylinders, oximeters for daily check-ups, and specialized first-aid kits for altitude sickness protection.

Exclusions Checklist

To maintain full transparency, the following items are not included in the standard package pricing:

  • Personal Trekking Gear: Heavy jackets, trekking poles, sleeping bags, and personal hiking boots.
  • Insurance: Personal travel insurance covering medical emergency and high-altitude evacuation.
  • Personal Expenses: Telephone bills, laundry charges, tips to guides/staff, and extra mineral water.
  • Pony / Mule Hire: Charges for individual pony riding or personal luggage carrying during walking segments.
  • Emergency Costs: Cost of helicopter rescue, hospitalizations, or itinerary deviations due to landslides or roadblocks.

General Tour Policies

  • Starting Point: All packages start and end at Delhi, Pithoragarh or Dharchula as specified.
  • Veg Only: On spiritual pilgrimage routes (Adi Kailash, Mt. Kailash), only pure vegetarian food is prepared and served.
  • Permits Document: Required documents must be submitted at least 15 days in advance.
  • Weather Policy: Routes may change dynamically based on administrative orders and local weather updates for safety.

Have Questions About Inclusions?

We are happy to customize what is included for your private group or corporate trekking teams. Speak directly with us.
