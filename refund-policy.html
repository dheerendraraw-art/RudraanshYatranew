Refund & Cancellation Policy

We believe in complete transparency. This policy is written in plain language so you know exactly what to expect if plans change. We operate in high-altitude, weather-sensitive mountain terrain — refund decisions reflect the genuine costs and risks involved in organising yatras to restricted border zones.

1. Cancellation by Traveller

All cancellation requests must be submitted in writing via email to info@rudraanshyatra.com or WhatsApp message to +91 7617617651. Cancellations will be effective from the date we receive your written request.

Verbal cancellations are not accepted. Please ensure you receive a written acknowledgement from our team confirming the cancellation.

2. Refund Schedule

Refunds are calculated based on the number of days remaining before the confirmed tour departure date:

Days Before Departure Refund Amount Deduction
30 days or more 75% of total tour cost 25% retained (admin + permit costs)
15 to 29 days 50% of total tour cost 50% retained
7 to 14 days 25% of total tour cost 75% retained
Less than 7 days No refund 100% retained
Advance / Booking deposit Non-refundable Always retained regardless of cancellation date

Why are deductions applied? When you book a yatra with us, costs are committed upfront: government permit fees, advance payments to homestays, vehicle bookings, and staff coordination. These costs are non-recoverable even if you cancel well in advance.

3. Cancellation by Rudraansh Yatra

Rudraansh Yatra reserves the right to cancel a tour in the following circumstances:

  • Minimum group size not met (for group departures) — you will be offered a date change or full refund
  • Severe adverse weather, natural disaster, or force majeure events making the tour unsafe or impossible
  • Government-imposed restrictions on border zone access
  • Failure by the traveller to complete balance payment by the due date

If Rudraansh Yatra cancels a tour for reasons within our control, you will receive a full refund of all amounts paid, excluding any government fees that are non-recoverable. Refunds will be processed within 7 business days.

If cancellation is due to force majeure or circumstances beyond our control, we will refund all recoverable costs. Non-recoverable costs (government permits, pre-paid third-party services) cannot be refunded and will be transparently communicated to you.

4. No-Show Policy

A "no-show" occurs when a traveller fails to appear at the designated assembly point on the departure date without any prior written cancellation notice.

  • No-shows are treated as a cancellation with less than 7 days' notice
  • No refund will be issued for no-shows
  • The full tour cost will be forfeited

If you are delayed due to a genuine emergency (road accident, medical emergency, flight delay), please contact us immediately at +91 7617617651. We will assess the situation on a case-by-case basis and make reasonable accommodation where possible.

5. Trip Curtailment Mid-Tour

If a traveller chooses to discontinue a tour after it has commenced (for personal reasons, illness, or change of mind), the following applies:

  • No refund will be issued for unused tour services from the point of curtailment onward
  • Rudraansh Yatra will arrange safe return transport to the nearest base camp or highway at the traveller's expense
  • If curtailment is due to a medical emergency, we will assist in arranging evacuation at the traveller's cost; please refer to your travel insurance policy
  • If curtailment is due to Rudraansh Yatra's failure (e.g., vehicle breakdown, accommodation failure), a proportional refund for unused services will be provided

6. Non-Refundable Costs

The following costs are non-refundable under all circumstances:

  • Inner Line Permit fees paid to government authorities (SDM office, Dharchula) — once applied, these cannot be recovered
  • Non-refundable booking advance (30–50% of total cost) paid at the time of booking confirmation
  • Third-party services already paid and non-recoverable (helicopter bookings, specific homestay advance payments)
  • Payment gateway processing charges (typically 2–3% of transaction value) are not refunded
  • Costs incurred for any services already delivered before the cancellation date

7. Refund Process & Timeline

  1. Submit your cancellation request in writing to info@rudraanshyatra.com with your booking reference number
  2. Our team will acknowledge receipt of your request within 24 hours
  3. We will calculate the refund amount based on the schedule above and communicate it to you within 48 hours
  4. Approved refunds will be processed to your original payment method within 7–10 business days
  5. Bank transfer refunds may take an additional 3–5 business days to reflect in your account depending on your bank

Refunds are issued to the same payment source used for the original booking (UPI ID, bank account, or credit/debit card). Refunds to a different account require additional verification.

8. Date Change & Rescheduling

We understand that travel plans can change. We offer one complimentary date change per booking subject to availability, provided the request is received:

  • 30 or more days before the original departure date — free of charge
  • 15 to 29 days before departure — a rescheduling fee of ₹1,500 per person applies
  • Less than 15 days before departure — treated as a cancellation; standard cancellation policy applies

Date changes are subject to availability of slots, permits, and accommodation for the new dates. Rescheduled tours are priced at the rate applicable on the new travel date, which may differ from the original booking price.

9. Travel Insurance Recommendation

High-altitude yatras to restricted border zones carry inherent risks from weather, terrain, and altitude. We strongly recommend that all travellers purchase comprehensive travel insurance before booking, covering:

  • Trip cancellation and curtailment
  • Medical expenses including high-altitude sickness treatment
  • Emergency helicopter evacuation from mountain terrain
  • Baggage loss or delay
  • Personal accident cover

Travel insurance is the most effective way to protect yourself financially against unexpected cancellations, medical emergencies, and evacuation costs. Rudraansh Yatra is not responsible for insured losses that should be claimed through your travel insurance provider.
