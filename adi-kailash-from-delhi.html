Delhi Package — Complete Door-to-Door Yatra

The Delhi package is our most convenient all-inclusive option — perfect for families, senior pilgrims, and groups who want a completely hassle-free experience without arranging their own travel to Kumaon. Our vehicle picks you up from Delhi/NCR and drops you back after the complete yatra.

Over 6 days and 5 nights, you complete the full sacred circuit: overnight transit to Pithoragarh, the complete Vyas Valley pilgrimage (Adi Kailash at 4,570m + Om Parvat at 4,246m), a day of Pithoragarh exploration, and a spiritual return via Jageshwar Dham, Chitai Golu Devta, and Kainchi Dham — all without changing vehicles.

Prefer taking a train or starting directly in Kumaon? Check our train-connected Kathgodam Package (6D · ₹30,000) or our direct local Pithoragarh Package (3D · ₹18,000). For comprehensive mythology, route maps, and preparation guides, read our Master Adi Kailash & Om Parvat Guide.

🚗 Pick-up Point: Your Delhi/NCR address or a designated pick-up point in Delhi. Evening departure (approx. 7–9 PM) to reach Pithoragarh the next morning. No train booking needed.

6-Day Itinerary — Delhi Route

Day 1 (Night): Delhi → Pithoragarh (Overnight Drive)

Day 1

Our AC Tempo Traveller departs from your designated Delhi pick-up point in the evening (approx. 7–9 PM). Drive overnight via Moradabad – Bareilly – Tanakpur – Champawat route through the scenic Kumaon foothills. Arrive at Pithoragarh by early morning (approx. 6–8 AM). Check in to hotel. Rest, freshen up, and have breakfast. Evening briefing by our yatra team on ILP, altitude protocol, and the itinerary ahead. Overnight stay in Pithoragarh hotel.

Distance: ~520 km | Drive: ~10–12 hrs (overnight) | Night halt: Pithoragarh hotel

Day 2: Pithoragarh → Nabi / Gunji via Dharchula & Chialekh Pass

Day 2

Depart early morning from Pithoragarh in our 4x4 Bolero. Drive along the stunning Kali River route through Dharchula (915m), clearing ITBP checkpoints with your Inner Line Permit. Ascend through the dramatic Chialekh Pass into the pristine Vyas Valley. Arrive at the high-altitude tribal village of Nabi or Gunji (3,200m) by late afternoon. Check in to authentic Bhotiya homestay. Rest and acclimatize at altitude. Dinner and overnight.

Distance: ~180 km | Drive: ~8 hrs | Night halt: Nabi/Gunji homestay (3,200m)

Day 3: Jyolingkong — Adi Kailash Darshan (4,570m)

Day 3

Drive from Nabi/Gunji to Jyolingkong (4,570m / 15,000 ft). Witness the majestic Adi Kailash (Chhota Kailash) — the revered twin of Mt. Kailash — towering above the sacred valley. Visit Parvati Sarovar lake, Gauri Kund, and the ancient Parvati temple. Perform puja rituals with our local pandit. Absorb the divine stillness of this remote Himalayan sanctuary. Return to Nabi/Gunji for dinner and overnight rest.

Max altitude: 4,570m | Night halt: Nabi/Gunji

Day 4: Om Parvat Darshan (4,246m) → Return to Pithoragarh

Day 4

Early morning drive to Nabidhang (4,246m). Witness the miraculous, naturally formed "ॐ" snow symbol etched on Om Parvat's face by divine geometry — one of the most photographed and spiritually charged sights in the Himalayas. After this culminating darshan, begin the long descent drive through Chialekh Pass, past Dharchula, and back to Pithoragarh. Arrive by evening. Overnight at hotel in Pithoragarh.

Max altitude: 4,246m | Night halt: Pithoragarh hotel

Day 5: Pithoragarh Exploration — Mini Kashmir

Day 5

A relaxed day in Pithoragarh — Uttarakhand's "Mini Kashmir." Visit the British-era Pithoragarh Fort with panoramic Himalayan views, the sacred Mostamanu Temple, Chandak Hills viewpoint, and the Simalgair Bazaar for Himalayan handicrafts, pashmina shawls, and local spices. Optional visits to Dhwaj Temple or Kapileshwar Mahadev Cave. Evening rest at hotel. Overnight in Pithoragarh.

Night halt: Pithoragarh hotel

Day 6: Return to Delhi via Jageshwar, Chitai Golu & Kainchi Dham

Day 6

Depart Pithoragarh after breakfast. Drive via Almora to the ancient cluster of 124 stone Shiva temples at Jageshwar Dham — a UNESCO tentative heritage site. Seek blessings at the sacred bell-laden Chitai Golu Devta temple. Visit Kainchi Dham — the world-famous ashram of Neem Karoli Baba (revered by Steve Jobs, Mark Zuckerberg, and millions of devotees). Continue the drive back to Delhi, arriving by late night. Yatra ends at your Delhi drop-off point.

Distance: ~520 km | Drive: ~12 hrs | Yatra concludes in Delhi

Inner Line Permit — We Handle Everything

An Inner Line Permit (ILP) is mandatory for all Indian nationals on the Adi Kailash route (restricted border zone). We manage the complete application through the Dharchula SDM office — you simply provide documents 15 days in advance.

Documents needed (submit 15 days in advance):

  • Aadhaar Card — colour copy (both sides)
  • Police Verification Certificate (PCC) from local police station
  • 4 recent passport-size photographs (physical)
  • Medical fitness certificate from registered MBBS doctor

We submit, track approval, and carry permits for all ITBP checkpoints. Read our full guide: ILP for Adi Kailash 2026.
