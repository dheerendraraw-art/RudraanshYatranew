Panchachuli Base Camp Trek

Trek through the pristine Darma Valley to the snout of the Meola Glacier, right under the shadows of the five majestic peaks of Panchachuli.

6 Days / 5 Nights Moderate Pithoragarh Start

Under the Shadows of the Five Chariots of Fire

The Panchachuli range consists of five snow-capped Himalayan peaks lying in the Eastern Kumaon region. This trek leads you along the Dhauliganga river into Darma Valley, crossing remote border villages, green birch forests, and glacial streams to stand right at the base of these towering giants at 4,260 meters.

Because this trek ventures close to the Tibetan border, it remains pristine and rich in tribal heritage. As native guides from Pithoragarh, we manage all forest department permit fees, provide high-quality camping gear, arrange rugged 4x4 Boleros, and introduce you to authentic tribal homestays (see what's included for details).

The trek to Panchachuli Base Camp is a feast for the eyes, combining the raw power of the Meola Glacier with alpine meadows and the unique culture of the Darma Valley community.

  • Snout of Meola Glacier: Stand directly before the massive ice structures.
  • Five Peaks: Admire the majestic summits ranging from 6,334m to 6,904m.
  • Darma Valley Flora: Walk through alpine meadows filled with wildflowers, pine, and birch trees.
  • Rung Tribal Culture: Learn about the unique border traditions, architecture, and food.

Day-Wise Travel Plan

Day 1: Pithoragarh to Dharchula / Dhar

Day 1

Drive from Pithoragarh base via Dharchula to Dhar village (approx. 120 km, 5-6 hours) in the lower Darma Valley. This drive offers spectacular views of the border river Kali. Check in at guest house. Overnight stay in Dhar (Altitude: 2,100m).

Day 2: Dhar to Urthing

Day 2

Begin the first segment of trekking. Walk 10 km along the roaring Dhauliganga River to the campsite at Urthing. The trail passes through narrow gorges and beautiful waterfalls. Overnight in alpine tents (Altitude: 2,438m).

Day 3: Urthing to Naaling

Day 3

Trek 12 km from Urthing to Naaling village. The trail gets steeper, winding past high birch trees and opening up to views of snow peaks. Check in at a local homestay in Naaling. Experience traditional food. Overnight stay in Naaling (Altitude: 2,900m).

Day 4: Naaling to Son / Panchachuli Base Camp

Day 4

Trek 10 km from Naaling via Son village to the holy Panchachuli Base Camp (3,475m) directly under the glacier snout. Marvel at the five massive peaks towering right above you. Set up camp. Overnight in alpine tents (Altitude: 3,475m).

Day 5: Explore Base Camp & Return to Naaling

Day 5

Wake up early to catch the golden sunrise lighting up the five peaks. Take walks close to the Meola Glacier snout. After breakfast, begin the descent back to Naaling village (10 km). Enjoy a warm meal and rest. Overnight in Naaling homestay.

Day 6: Return to Pithoragarh

Day 6

Trek back from Naaling to Dhar (approx. 22 km downhill). Meet our 4x4 Boleros at Dhar and drive back to Pithoragarh. The trek concludes in Pithoragarh by evening.

Permits & Environmental Safety

Darma Valley lies close to the international border, requiring Inner Line permissions. We handle these at the Pithoragarh or Dharchula SDM offices directly. We practice **Strict Leave No Trace (LNT)** policies on this route:

  • Bio-degradable waste: We collect all plastics, tins, and wrappers and pack them back to Pithoragarh for disposal.
  • Homestay support: We source organic grains, potatoes, and vegetables directly from Darma valley farmers.
  • Hydration: Water is sourced from pure glacial streams and filtered. No packaged plastic bottles are allowed.
