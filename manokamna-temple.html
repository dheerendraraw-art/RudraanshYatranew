Manokamna Temple

The sacred wish-fulfilling shrine in the Himalayan cedar forests of Pithoragarh — a divine halt on the Adi Kailash Yatra route.

What is Manokamna Temple?

Manokamna Temple is a sacred Hindu shrine dedicated to a wish-fulfilling deity, located in the dense Himalayan deodar cedar forests of Pithoragarh district, Uttarakhand, India. Situated on the arterial mountain road leading towards the Vyas Valley and Adi Kailash, the temple is a deeply revered waypoint for pilgrims undertaking the Adi Kailash & Om Parvat Yatra. The word Manokamna in Sanskrit means "the one who fulfils the wishes of the heart," making it a must-visit for devotees seeking divine blessings before ascending to the highest altitudes of the sacred Himalayan circuit.

The temple is surrounded by thick cedar and oak forests at approximately 1,800–2,200 metres above sea level. The serene mountain ambience, the sound of bells, and the scent of incense make this shrine a spiritually uplifting experience that prepares pilgrims for the higher sacred darshans of Adi Kailash and Om Parvat ahead.

History & Spiritual Significance

The Manokamna Temple in Pithoragarh holds deep roots in Kumaoni folk tradition and Himalayan Hindu pilgrimage culture. For centuries, local villagers and spiritual seekers have sought the blessings of the presiding deity before embarking on arduous high-altitude journeys into the Vyas Valley border region near Tibet and Nepal.

The name Manokamna — derived from Sanskrit mano (mind/heart) and kamna (desire/wish) — reflects the temple's core spiritual purpose: to help devotees align their innermost wishes with divine will. Pilgrims pray here before the ascent, believing divine protection is granted for the full yatra. The temple complex features stone-built Kumaoni architecture, colourful prayer flags, brass bells, and a peaceful sanctum sanctorum surrounded by ancient Himalayan trees. Local belief holds that the deity specifically protects high-altitude travellers and border pilgrims who seek her blessings sincerely.

How to Reach

  1. From Pithoragarh: Drive along the main road towards Dharchula. Manokamna Temple is located on or near this route through the mountain forest zones. Distance: approx. 20–40 km from Pithoragarh town centre.
  2. Via Rudraansh Yatra Package: All Adi Kailash packages include a stop at the temple enroute. Your 4x4 Mahindra Bolero will stop there — no separate arrangement needed.
  3. From Kathgodam: Take an overnight train to Kathgodam, then drive via Almora and Pithoragarh (approx. 260 km, 6 hrs). The temple is encountered on the onward drive towards Vyas Valley.
  4. Nearest Airport: Pantnagar Airport (PGH), approx. 210 km. Taxi via Haldwani–Almora–Pithoragarh takes 4–5 hours.

Best Time to Visit

The ideal time to visit Manokamna Temple is during the Adi Kailash Yatra season: May to June (pre-monsoon) and September to October (post-monsoon). During these months mountain roads are clear, weather is favourable, and the forests are lush. Monsoon months (July–August) can make mountain roads treacherous. Winter months (November–March) bring heavy snowfall, restricting access to the Vyas Valley route.
