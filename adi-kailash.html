Spiritual Pilgrimage

Adi Kailash & Om Parvat Yatra

A sacred journey to the heart of Vyas Valley, witnessing the naturally formed 'Om' and the replication of Mt. Kailash.

6 Days / 5 Nights Moderate to Spiritual Delhi / Dehradun Start

Explore the Sacred Vyas Valley

The Adi Kailash and Om Parvat Yatra is a lifetime spiritual pilgrimage deep within Uttarakhand's borderlands. Traversing the beautiful Vyas Valley, this route leads directly under the eyes of Mt. Adi Kailash (4,750m) and the majestic Om Parvat, where snow forms a natural 'Om' symbol.

As hyper-local experts based in Pithoragarh, we manage the entire ground operation directly. We process official Inner Line Permits, guide you through security checkpoints, arrange 4x4 Mahindra Boleros, and host you in local homestays supporting remote border families (view what's included in our package).

Sacred Pilgrimage Highlights

Adi Kailash, often referred to as Chhota Kailash, is a majestic peak mirroring the spiritual aura of Mount Kailash. The journey is an adventure filled with high waterfalls, winding river borders, dense pine forests, and traditional Kumaoni villages.

  • Vyas Valley Culture: Experience hospitality in Gunji & Nabi villages.
  • Parvati Sarovar & Gauri Kund: Perform holy rituals and puja at the banks of sacred water bodies.
  • Om Parvat Darshan: Stand before the natural marvel at Nabidhang where snow shapes the divine 'Om'.
  • Jageshwar Dham & Chitai Golu Devta: Visit the ancient cluster of 124 stone temples and the sacred temple of bells.
  • Kainchi Dham: Seek blessings at the famous spiritual ashram of Neem Karoli Baba.

Day-Wise Travel Plan

Day 1: Delhi / Dehradun to Pithoragarh via Tanakpur

Day 1

Embark on your journey from Delhi or Dehradun to the base town of Pithoragarh via Tanakpur. Transition from the plains to the scenic foothills of the Kumaon Himalayas. Check in at your hotel, relax, and prepare for the sacred yatra ahead. Overnight stay in Pithoragarh.

Day 2: Pithoragarh to Nabi / Gunji

Day 2

Depart early morning from Pithoragarh. Drive past Dharchula, ascending into the glorious Vyas Valley via the iconic Chialekh Pass. Arrive in the high-altitude frontier villages of Nabi or Gunji. Check in to an authentic local homestay. Overnight stay in Nabi / Gunji (Altitude: 3,200m).

Day 3: Nabi Village to Jyolingkong (Adi Kailash Darshan)

Day 3

Drive to Jyolingkong (4,570m) to witness the spectacular, holy Adi Kailash peak. Visit the sacred waters of Gauri Kund and the temple at Parvati Sarovar. Perform sacred puja rituals, absorb the divine energies, and return to Nabi or Gunji village for the night. Overnight in local homestay.

Day 4: Om Parvat - Mt. Kailash Viewpoints - Pithoragarh

Day 4

Drive to Nabhidhang (4,246m) to witness the miraculous, naturally etched snow "ॐ" on Om Parvat and view the distant Mt. Kailash viewpoints. After this ultimate double darshan, begin the long descent drive all the way back to the base town of Pithoragarh. Overnight stay in Pithoragarh.

Day 5: Pithoragarh Excursion / Exploration

Day 5

Enjoy a relaxed day exploring Pithoragarh (often called Mini Kashmir). Visit local historic attractions like the Pithoragarh Fort, the sacred Mostamanu Temple, Chandak Hills, or shop for local Himalayan handicrafts. Overnight stay in Pithoragarh.

Day 6: Return to Delhi / Dehradun via Kathgodam

Day 6

Depart from Pithoragarh after breakfast. Drive down to Kathgodam, and continue your journey back to Delhi or Dehradun, carrying home the spiritual blessings, divine protection, and unforgettable memories of the sacred peaks.

Inner Line Permit Guidelines

Vyas Valley borders China and Nepal, meaning an **Inner Line Permit (ILP)** is legally required. We handle all approvals at Dharchula SDM office directly, but you must submit these documents to us **15 days prior to yatra date**:

  • Identity Proof: Clean scanned Aadhaar Card.
  • Police Verification Certificate (PCC): Issued by your local police station.
  • Medical Certificate: Signed by a registered doctor declaring high altitude fitness.
  • Photos: 4 recent passport size photographs.

High Altitude & AMS Safety

Jyolingkong sits at 4,570 meters. Proper care is essential to avoid Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS):

Acclimatization

Our itinerary has built-in gradual ascents. We stay in Dharchula (915m) before climbing to Gunji (3,200m).

Hydration

Drink 4�5 liters of water daily. Dehydration accelerates mountain sickness. Avoid alcohol during the climb.

Medical Backup

Every vehicle carries medical oxygen. Guides check oxygen saturation levels daily using pulse oximeters.
