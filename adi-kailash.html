The Sacred Vyas Valley Pilgrimage

The Adi Kailash and Om Parvat Yatra is one of the most sacred spiritual pilgrimages in the Indian Himalayas. Located in the remote Vyas Valley of Pithoragarh district, Uttarakhand, near the confluence of India, Nepal, and Tibet, this sacred circuit brings devotees to the earthly replica of Mt. Kailash (4,570m) and the divine snow "ॐ" etched naturally on Om Parvat (4,246m).

As a direct ground operator headquartered in Pithoragarh, Rudraansh Yatra manages every detail locally: official Inner Line Permits (ILP) with SDM and ITBP clearances, robust 4x4 Mahindra Boleros driven by native valley drivers, warm Bhotiya tribal homestays, and portable medical oxygen backup for senior and family pilgrims.

Day-by-Day Pilgrimage Plan

Day 1: Delhi / Kathgodam to Pithoragarh Base

Day 1 · 1,814m

Depart from Delhi (evening pickup in AC vehicle) or Kathgodam railway station. Drive through the scenic Kumaon hills transitioning to the picturesque base town of Pithoragarh. Check in at hotel, complete ILP permit verification briefing with our team, and rest for the mountain ascent. Overnight in Pithoragarh.

Day 2: Pithoragarh to Nabi / Gunji via Chialekh Pass

Day 2 · 3,200m

Early morning departure in 4x4 Bolero. Drive along the Kali River past Dharchula to the ITBP checkpoint. Ascend through the legendary Chialekh Pass into the pristine alpine heights of Vyas Valley. Arrive in Nabi or Gunji tribal village. Settle into our authentic Bhotiya tribal homestay and acclimatize to the 3,200m altitude. Hot organic dinner and overnight stay.

Day 3: Jyolingkong Base · Adi Kailash Darshan & Parvati Sarovar

Day 3 · 4,570m

The core sacred day! Drive up to Jyolingkong (4,570m) to witness the divine peak of Adi Kailash rising dramatically against the Himalayan sky. Visit the sacred waters of Parvati Sarovar and Gauri Kund temple. Perform holy puja, meditate in the divine vibrations, and capture unforgettable memories. Return to Nabi/Gunji homestay for a warm evening and rest.

Day 4: Nabidhang · Om Parvat Darshan & Return to Pithoragarh

Day 4 · 4,246m

Early morning drive to Nabidhang viewpoint (4,246m) to witness the miraculous, naturally etched snow "ॐ" on the face of Om Parvat. After this ultimate double darshan, begin the descent drive back through Vyas Valley and Dharchula to the base town of Pithoragarh. Check in to hotel, relax, and celebrate the successful pilgrimage. Overnight in Pithoragarh.

Day 5: Pithoragarh Local Exploration & Relaxation

Day 5 · 1,814m

A relaxed day exploring the historic sights of Pithoragarh (Mini Kashmir). Visit the 18th-century Pithoragarh Fort with panoramic valley views, Mostamanu Temple, Chandak Hills, or shop for authentic Kumaoni woolens and organic honey in Simalgair Bazaar. Overnight stay in Pithoragarh.

Day 6: Return via Jageshwar Dham, Chitai Golu & Kainchi Dham

Day 6 · Return

Depart Pithoragarh after breakfast. Drive via Almora to visit the 124 ancient stone temples of Jageshwar Dham, seek blessings at the sacred bell temple of Chitai Golu Devta, and visit Neem Karoli Baba's Kainchi Dham. Proceed onward to Kathgodam railway station or Delhi NCR drop-off, concluding a blessed yatra.

Day 1: Pithoragarh to Nabi / Gunji Homestay

Day 1 · 3,200m

Early morning departure from Rudraansh Yatra office in Pithoragarh. Drive past Dharchula and ascending via Chialekh Pass into Vyas Valley. Arrive at Nabi or Gunji tribal homestay by afternoon. Rest, hydrate, and acclimatize. Overnight in Bhotiya homestay.

Day 2: Adi Kailash & Om Parvat Double Darshan

Day 2 · 4,570m

Drive to Jyolingkong (4,570m) for Adi Kailash & Parvati Sarovar darshan. Later, proceed to Nabidhang (4,246m) for the sacred Om Parvat snow "ॐ" darshan. Return to Nabi/Gunji homestay for dinner and overnight stay.

Day 3: Nabi / Gunji to Pithoragarh Base

Day 3 · Conclude

After breakfast, depart Nabi/Gunji and drive down through Vyas Valley and Dharchula. Conclude the yatra at our Pithoragarh office by afternoon with sacred prasad and memories.

Inner Line Permit (ILP) Process

Vyas Valley is a protected border zone along the India-China and India-Nepal boundaries. An official Inner Line Permit (ILP) issued by the SDM Office in Dharchula is legally mandatory for all Indian travelers. Rudraansh Yatra handles 100% of the permit processing for all booked yatris.

Required Documents (Submit 15 Days Prior)

1. Aadhaar Card Clear, legible color photocopy of both front and back on a single sheet.
2. Police Verification Certificate (PCC) Character certificate issued by your local permanent address police station (takes 7-10 days).
3. 4 Passport Photographs Recent passport-size color photographs on a crisp white background (3.5 x 4.5 cm).
4. Medical Fitness Certificate Signed and stamped by an MBBS-registered doctor certifying physical fitness for high altitude above 4,000m.
How to Submit:

Simply send high-resolution digital scans to our WhatsApp at +91 7617617651 or email info@rudraanshyatra.com. We take care of the rest.

High Altitude & AMS Safety Guidelines

Jyolingkong sits at 4,570 meters (15,000 ft) and Nabidhang at 4,246 meters. High altitude requires sensible acclimatization and medical awareness to avoid Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS).

Gradual Acclimatization

Our itinerary features built-in gradual ascents — resting at Dharchula (915m) and overnight stay in Gunji/Nabi (3,200m) before ascending to the 4,570m base.

Hydration & Diet

Drink 3.5 to 4.5 liters of clean water daily. Eat light, carbohydrate-rich vegetarian meals. Completely avoid alcohol and smoking during the high-altitude ascent.

Portable Oxygen Backup

Every Rudraansh Yatra vehicle carries certified medical oxygen canisters and digital pulse oximeters to monitor SpO2 levels every morning and evening.

Senior Citizen Protocols

For pilgrims aged 60+, our vehicles park closest to the temple viewpoints. Pony support is arranged for the gentle 150m walking stretch at Parvati Sarovar.
