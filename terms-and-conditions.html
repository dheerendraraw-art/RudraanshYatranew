5. Health & Fitness Requirements

  • All yatras and treks involve travel to high altitudes (above 3,000m/10,000ft). Travellers must be in good physical health
  • For Adi Kailash & Om Parvat Yatra (reaching 4,750m), a medical fitness certificate from a registered doctor is strongly recommended for travellers above 60 years or with known cardiovascular, respiratory, or diabetic conditions
  • Rudraansh Yatra reserves the right to refuse service or require a traveller to descend if, in the guide's professional judgment, the traveller's health poses a risk to themselves or the group
  • In such cases, no refund will be provided for unused services beyond what is recoverable
  • Every vehicle carries portable oxygen cylinders, pulse oximeters, and basic first-aid kits
  • Travellers with severe Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS) will be evacuated by the fastest safe route; any additional evacuation costs beyond standard trip costs are the traveller's responsibility

  • Rudraansh Yatra is not responsible for permit rejections by government authorities due to incomplete or inaccurate documentation provided by the traveller
