Why Rudraansh Yatra

The Local Pioneer Advantage

We are native explorers of Pithoragarh who run all tours directly. By avoiding middle-agents, we guarantee authentic, safe, and community-supported travel.

100% Direct Execution

We maintain our own fleet of 4x4 Mahindra Boleros and local support teams. We never resell packages, meaning you deal with us directly from start to finish.

The Green Travel Pledge

We follow strict environmental ethics. We use zero single-use plastic, supply reusable steel bottles, and pack out all waste generated during our high-altitude climbs.

Supporting Local Homestays

We accommodate our guests in community-owned mountain homestays, ensuring that tourism funds directly benefit the remote families living on border frontiers.

Direct Local Ground Safeguard

Unlike ticketing portal sites, our office is physically located at 1st Floor Above Punetha Bookstore, Simailgair Bazaar, Pithoragarh. In case of unexpected weather, permit delays, or emergencies, we solve issues instantly on the spot.
