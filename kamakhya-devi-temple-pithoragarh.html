Kamakhya Devi Temple, Pithoragarh

Sacred hilltop Shakti temple in Pithoragarh surrounded by rhododendron forests, with sweeping panoramic Himalayan views — visited on the Adi Kailash Yatra local day.

What is Kamakhya Devi Temple, Pithoragarh?

Kamakhya Devi Temple in Pithoragarh, Uttarakhand, is a sacred hilltop shrine dedicated to Goddess Kamakhya — one of the most potent manifestations of Shakti in Hindu tradition. Situated on a hilltop on the outskirts of Pithoragarh town, the temple is surrounded by beautiful rhododendron (buransh) and oak forests that burst into vibrant crimson bloom in spring. The hilltop setting grants visitors sweeping panoramic views of the surrounding Himalayan landscape, including distant snow peaks and the valleys of Pithoragarh district.

Kamakhya Devi Temple is a significant Shakti pitha for the communities of Pithoragarh and Kumaon, attracting both local devotees and pilgrims passing through on the Adi Kailash Yatra. During the 6-day Adi Kailash packages, the temple is a highlight of the Pithoragarh local sightseeing day (Day 5), offering a serene and spiritually charged morning visit.

History & Spiritual Significance

Goddess Kamakhya is one of the most ancient and powerful goddesses in the Hindu Shakta tradition. The name Kamakhya derives from Sanskrit roots meaning "the goddess of desire" — she is considered the supreme deity of Tantric Shakti worship, with the main temple at Guwahati, Assam, being among the most sacred Shakti peethas in India. The Pithoragarh temple is a regional manifestation of this powerful goddess, established to bring her divine presence and protection to the communities of the Kumaon Himalayas.

The temple in Pithoragarh is believed to have been established centuries ago by local Kumaoni kings and trading communities who venerated Goddess Kamakhya as a protectress of commerce, journeys, and the community. The location on a forested hilltop — overlooking the entire Soar Valley — was chosen to give the goddess a commanding divine vantage point over her domain. The rhododendron forests surrounding the temple are considered sacred and have never been cleared, preserving a pristine pocket of Himalayan biodiversity around the shrine.

How to Reach

  1. From Pithoragarh Town: The temple is located on a hill on the outskirts of Pithoragarh town, approximately 3–6 km from the town centre. Local taxis and autos ply this route.
  2. By Walking: A forest walking trail connects the town to the temple through the rhododendron woods — a peaceful 1–2 hour hike through pristine Himalayan forest.
  3. Via Rudraansh Yatra 6-Day Package: Kamakhya Devi Temple is included in the Day 5 Pithoragarh local sightseeing schedule of the Kathgodam and Delhi 6-day packages.
  4. Nearest Airport: Pantnagar Airport (PGH), approx. 200 km. Taxi takes 4–5 hours via Haldwani and Almora.

Best Time to Visit

Kamakhya Devi Temple is beautiful year-round, but particularly stunning in March–April when the surrounding rhododendron forests bloom in vivid crimson. The Adi Kailash Yatra months of May–June and September–October are the ideal time for pilgrims to include this temple in their visit. Early mornings offer the best Himalayan panoramic views from the hilltop before clouds develop.
