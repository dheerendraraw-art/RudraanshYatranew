Alpine Adventure

Khaliya Top Meadow Trek

An alpine meadow trek offering a panoramic view of the Panchachuli peaks. Perfect for beginners and winter snow lovers.

4 Days / 3 Nights Easy to Moderate Pithoragarh Start

Ascend to the Alpine Paradise of Munsiyari

Khaliya Top is a massive alpine meadow (Bugyal) situated near the picturesque town of Munsiyari, elevated at 3,500 meters. The trek takes you through dense forests of rhododendron and oak, opening up to clear grasslands that offer views of Panchachuli, Nanda Devi, Hardeol, and Rajrambha peaks.

This trek is perfect for first-timers, families, and winter travelers looking to experience snow-laden trails. As Pithoragarh natives, we coordinate the complete transport, local homestays in Munsiyari, camping permits, and provide highly experienced guides who know the meadows inside out.

Trek Highlights

Khaliya Top is renowned for its ease of access and massive visual rewards. In spring, the forest path is ablaze with red rhododendron blooms, while winters turn the meadow into a vast skiing ground.

  • 360-Degree Himalayan Vista: Spot the Panchachuli and Johar valley peaks closely.
  • Alpine Forest Trail: Walk under oak, pine, and scarlet rhododendron trees.
  • Beginner-Friendly: A comfortable trek with a total trekking distance of only 8 km.
  • Community Tourism: Enjoy local food and stays in Munsiyari village.

Day-Wise Travel Plan

Day 1: Pithoragarh to Munsiyari

Day 1

Drive from Pithoragarh to Munsiyari (approx. 130 km, 5-6 hours) via Thal and the Birthi Falls. Munsiyari is a gorgeous hill station at the foot of Panchachuli. Check in at guest house. Evening stroll in local markets. Overnight stay in Munsiyari (Altitude: 2,298m).

Day 2: Munsiyari to Khaliya Top Campsite

Day 2

Drive 6 km to Balati Bend (trek starting point). Begin a gradual 4 km hike through oak forests. The trail opens up into high-altitude meadows. Arrive at Khaliya Top alpine campsite by afternoon. Witness sunset over the peaks. Overnight in high-quality alpine tents (Altitude: 3,250m).

Day 3: Summit Day & Return to Munsiyari

Day 3

Early morning trek of 2 km to the Khaliya Top summit (3,500m) for sunrise. Get a closer view of the Panchachuli and Nanda Devi peaks. Spend time taking photos and breathing the crisp mountain air. Trek back down to Balati Bend and drive back to Munsiyari. Overnight stay in Munsiyari guest house.

Day 4: Munsiyari to Pithoragarh

Day 4

After a hearty breakfast, drive back from Munsiyari to Pithoragarh. The tour concludes by late afternoon upon arrival in Pithoragarh.

Permits & Winter Safety

Khaliya Top lies in the forest reserve area. All forest department permits and fee clearances are processed by us. For winter treks (December to February), the trail gets heavy snowfall:

  • Warm Wear: Layering is key as temperatures drop below freezing at night.
  • Grip: We provide microspikes or crampons for walking on hard winter ice.
  • Acclimatization: Staying in Munsiyari (2,300m) helps prevent altitude headaches before camping at 3,250m.
