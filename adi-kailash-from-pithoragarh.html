3-Day Itinerary — Pithoragarh Route

Day 1: Pithoragarh → Nabi / Gunji via Dharchula & Chialekh Pass

Day 1

Depart early morning (4–5 AM) from our Pithoragarh office. Drive along the Kali River route through Dharchula (915m), clearing ITBP checkpoints with your pre-arranged Inner Line Permit. Ascend through the dramatic Chialekh Pass and descend into Vyas Valley. Arrive at Nabi or Gunji village (3,200m) by afternoon. Check in to a Bhotiya tribal homestay. Light acclimatization walk. Dinner and overnight.

Distance: ~180 km | Drive time: ~8 hrs | Night halt: Nabi/Gunji (3,200m)

Day 2: Adi Kailash & Om Parvat — Sacred Double Darshan

Day 2

Morning: Drive to Jyolingkong (4,570m). Receive darshan of Adi Kailash (Chhota Kailash). Visit Parvati Sarovar, Gauri Kund, and the Parvati temple. Perform puja. Return to Nabi/Gunji.

Afternoon: Drive to Nabidhang (4,246m). Witness the miraculous "ॐ" snow symbol on Om Parvat. Return to homestay for dinner and overnight.

Max altitude: 4,570m | Night halt: Nabi/Gunji

Day 3: Return — Nabi / Gunji → Pithoragarh

Day 3

Early breakfast at homestay. Begin the return drive from Nabi/Gunji back through Chialekh Pass and Dharchula. Arrive at Pithoragarh by afternoon. Yatra concludes at our office. Carry home the blessings of the sacred peaks.

Distance: ~180 km | Drive time: ~8 hrs | Yatra ends in Pithoragarh. (Looking for return via Jageshwar Dham & Kainchi Dham? See our 6-day Kathgodam or Delhi packages.)

Inner Line Permit — We Handle Everything

An Inner Line Permit (ILP) is mandatory for all Indian nationals on the Adi Kailash route. We manage the complete application through the Dharchula SDM office.

Documents needed from you (submit 15 days in advance):

  • Aadhaar Card — colour copy (both sides)
  • Police Verification Certificate (PCC) from local police
  • 4 recent passport-size photographs (physical)
  • Medical fitness certificate from registered MBBS doctor

We submit, track, and carry your ILP for all ITBP checkpoints. Read our full guide: ILP for Adi Kailash 2026.
